# Promotional image prompts

These final assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool, then center-cropped and resized to the exact Chrome Web Store dimensions.

## Small promo tile

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: Chrome Web Store small promotional tile, 440x280 landscape

Primary request: Original, polished brand visual for a browser extension that creates live subtitles from tab audio.

Scene and subject: Full-bleed deep black and charcoal field with subtle depth; a clean front-facing abstract caption card made of three short white horizontal subtitle lines, surrounded by a restrained flowing audio waveform; small cyan, yellow, and green accents suggest distinct speakers.

Style and composition: Premium minimal 2D/3D hybrid graphic, crisp geometry, high contrast, centered bold symbol, large simple shapes, readable at half size, and clearly defined edges.

Constraints: No words, letters, logos, browser trademarks, badges, claims, watermark, screenshot, or detailed UI; square corners and full bleed.

## Marquee promo

Scene and subject: Full-bleed deep black and charcoal field with subtle depth; a clean front-facing abstract caption card made of three short white horizontal subtitle lines, integrated with a flowing audio waveform; cyan, yellow, and green accents suggest distinct speakers.

Style and composition: Premium minimal 2D/3D hybrid graphic, crisp geometry, high contrast, very wide and uncluttered, centered caption symbol, waveform extending horizontally, bold shapes, and safe breathing room.

Constraints: No words, letters, logos, browser trademarks, badges, claims, watermark, screenshot, or detailed UI; square corners and full bleed.
